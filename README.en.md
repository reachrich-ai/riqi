# RiQi

**An AI research assistant for China A-shares**

[Open RiQi](https://riqi.ai/) · [Product overview](https://reachrich.ai/riqi) · [Documentation](https://docs.reachrich.ai/) · [简体中文](README.md)

Bring your research question and documents into one conversation. Use RiQi to organise company information, discuss changes in the business, and question the evidence behind an answer. Return to the same conversation when you have a new announcement or a different interpretation to examine.

## Start with a specific question

Open [RiQi](https://riqi.ai/), follow the sign-in prompts and start a new analysis. Prepare the public annual-report material you want to discuss, and provide the relevant content with your question:

```text
Using the annual-report material
I provide, compare changes in
revenue and operating cash flow
over the last two reporting periods.

List the figures, units and sources.
Explain whether they moved together.
Separate reported facts from analysis.
List the questions still to check.
```

See the [question guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/ask.html) for operating steps.

Follow up on a specific point in the answer:

> You mentioned that changes in receivables could affect cash flow. Point to the supporting material and explain which other interpretations need checking.

When a new quarterly report is available, add the material and reporting period to the same conversation and revisit the earlier assessment.

## Work through a research question

### Organise company information

Specify the company, time horizon and main question before discussing the annual report, announcements or other material you have provided. Keep the source with the question so you can check it later.

[Company research walkthrough](https://docs.reachrich.ai/workflows/company-study.html)

### Question the answer

Check figures, reporting periods, citations and assumptions. Ask for the evidence behind competing explanations. If the material is insufficient, identify what to look for next.

[Read an analysis](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/read-answer.html) · [Follow up and revise](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/follow-up.html)

### Continue an existing study

Return to an existing conversation, add the new material and its date, and explain what has changed. Use a separate conversation for an unrelated company or task.

[Continue a research conversation](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/history.html)

## RiQi and ReachRich

[ReachRich](https://github.com/reachrich-ai/reachrich) provides market data, news and stock-screening tools. RiQi provides the conversation around a specific question and its supporting documents. Both use the same handbook; available features depend on account permissions.

Start with [ReachRich](https://reachrich.ai/) to review markets, search news or build a candidate list. Go directly to [RiQi](https://riqi.ai/) when you have a question and the material to discuss.
