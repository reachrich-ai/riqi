Discuss companies, market observations and research materials. Add evidence, compare explanations and revise a view in the same conversation, then return to that record as the research develops.

RiQi is the conversational research workspace in [ReachRich](https://github.com/reachrich-ai/reachrich). ReachRich provides market and screening tools; RiQi is for discussing questions and organizing follow-up work.

## Choose a starting point

| Task | Approach |
| :--- | :--- |
| Research a company | Specify the company, observation period and question, then discuss supplied disclosures or financial materials |
| Examine a conclusion | Follow up on a number, citation or assumption, and compare the evidence with other explanations |
| Continue earlier research | Reopen the conversation, identify new materials and dates, and separate new information from the earlier view |

## First session

1. Open [RiQi](https://riqi.ai/), follow the sign-in prompt and start a new analysis.
2. Specify the subject, question and time period. Provide the materials you want to discuss.
3. Read the response and follow up on specific evidence. Return to the same conversation when new information becomes available.

For an annual-report discussion, a useful prompt is:

> Using the annual report I provide, examine changes in this company's revenue and operating cash flow. Identify the reporting periods and sources, separate reported facts from your interpretation, and list questions that need further investigation.

[More prompt examples and usage instructions](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/ask.html).
