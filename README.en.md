# RiQi

RiQi is the A-share research assistant in the ReachRich product family. It helps organize company research materials, check sources and identify follow-up questions.

[Open RiQi](https://riqi.ai/) · [Product](https://reachrich.ai/riqi) · [User Guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/)

## Start with a clear question

Include the company and security identifier, research question, observation period, original materials and their dates, established facts, remaining assumptions, and what you want to compare next.
