RiQi is the AI research assistant from [ReachRich](https://github.com/reachrich-ai/reachrich), focused on China A-shares. Bring a question and the relevant documents into one conversation. Organise information, examine different explanations, and revisit your assessment as new material becomes available.

[Open RiQi](https://riqi.ai/) · [Product overview](https://reachrich.ai/riqi) · [Documentation](https://docs.reachrich.ai/) · [简体中文](README.md)

## Get started

1. Open RiQi, follow the sign-in prompts and start a new analysis.
2. Specify the company, reporting period and research question. Provide the material you want to discuss.
3. Read the answer, check figures and sources, then follow up on specific points.

For example, provide the relevant annual-report material with this question:

```text
Use the report I provide.
Compare revenue and operating
cash flow over two periods.

List figures, units, sources.
Explain the differences.
Separate facts from analysis.
List questions to check.
```

Follow up: "Could changes in receivables explain the cash-flow difference? Point to the supporting material and explain which other interpretations need checking."

## Continue your research

- **Organise company information**: specify the company, time horizon and main question. Keep the original disclosures with your research record. [Walkthrough](https://docs.reachrich.ai/workflows/company-study.html)
- **Check the evidence**: review reporting periods, figures, citations and assumptions. Distinguish reported facts from analysis. [Read an answer](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/read-answer.html)
- **Add material and follow up**: question a specific assessment and compare the evidence for different explanations. [Follow-up guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/follow-up.html)
- **Return to the same conversation**: add new disclosures, dates and changes to continue an existing study. [Conversation guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/history.html)

See the [question guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/riqi/ask.html) for more examples. Start with [ReachRich](https://reachrich.ai/) if you first need market data, news or a candidate list.
